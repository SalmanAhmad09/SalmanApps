Fox Reader — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 3, 2026 · Last Updated: April 3, 2026

Fox Reader ("the App," "we," "our," or "us") is an iOS e-book reader application developed by Salman Ahmad. This Privacy Policy describes how Fox Reader collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

Fox Reader stores the following data that you create or provide while using the App:

  • Imported Books: EPUB and PDF files you import into the App, stored locally on your device.
  • Reading Progress: Your current position, chapter, and percentage completion for each book.
  • Highlights and Notes: Text highlights (with color preferences) and personal notes you create within books.
  • Ratings and Reviews: Star ratings and category-based ratings (writing quality, personal impact, originality, etc.) you assign to books.
  • Tags and Collections: Custom tags, collections, and organizational structures you create for your library.
  • Vocabulary Words: Words you save to your vocabulary list, including mastery levels.
  • Reading Logs: Manual reading session entries including duration, pages read, and optional notes.
  • Book Tracking Data: Books you track (to-read, reading, completed, paused, did-not-finish), including start/finish dates, ratings, and personal notes.
  • Goodreads Import Data: If you choose to import a Goodreads CSV export, the App parses this file locally on your device to import your book data. The CSV file is not transmitted to any server.

All of the above data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. If you have iCloud enabled, this data may sync to your personal iCloud account (see Section 4.5).

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Analytics: We collect anonymous, non-identifying usage data through Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used and to improve the experience. This includes events such as: books imported (file type and word count), reading sessions (duration, words read, average reading speed), features used (summaries, vocabulary games, book tracking), subscription interactions (paywall views, subscription starts), and app performance metrics (book load times). These analytics are not linked to your identity.
  • Reader Preferences: Your chosen font, theme, reading speed (words per minute), and display settings are stored locally using UserDefaults.

1.3 Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal Identification: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information. There is no user account, registration, or login.
  • Location Data: We do not access or collect your location.
  • Contacts, Photos, or Calendar: We do not access your contacts, photo library, or calendar.
  • Advertising Identifiers: We do not collect or use Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA). Advertising is disabled in the App.
  • Cross-Site Tracking: We do not track you across other apps or websites. Our Privacy Manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) explicitly declares NSPrivacyTracking as false with no tracking domains.
  • Book Content: The text content of your books is never transmitted to any external server. All text processing (including AI summaries) occurs entirely on your device.

1.4 Camera Access

The App requests camera permission solely for scanning book barcodes (ISBN lookup) when adding books to your tracking list. Camera data is processed in real time for barcode detection only; no images or video are captured, stored, or transmitted.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above for the following purposes:

  • Providing Core Functionality: Rendering your books, tracking reading progress, displaying highlights and notes, and managing your library.
  • Personalization: Applying your preferred reading settings (font, theme, speed) across your reading experience.
  • Reading Statistics: Calculating and displaying your reading speed, session history, and completion data locally on your device.
  • Book Discovery: Fetching book metadata (titles, authors, descriptions, cover images) from public book databases when you search for or add books to your tracking list.
  • Author News: Displaying relevant news articles about authors in your library.
  • AI-Powered Summaries: Generating book summaries using Apple's on-device language model (see Section 3).
  • Analytics: Understanding aggregate usage patterns to improve the App's features and performance. Analytics data is anonymous and not linked to your identity.
  • Subscription Management: Verifying your subscription status to unlock premium features.

3. AI and Data Processing

3.1 On-Device AI Processing

Fox Reader uses Apple's Foundation Models framework to provide AI-powered features, including:

  • Book Summaries: Generating concise bullet-point summaries of book content.
  • Genre Classification: Automatically categorizing books into genres based on their content and metadata.

All AI processing occurs entirely on your device. Book text is processed by Apple's on-device language model and is never sent to external AI services, cloud-based language models, or any third-party API. No book content leaves your device for AI processing.

3.2 Rule-Based Classification

In addition to on-device AI, the App uses rule-based keyword matching and scoring algorithms for genre classification. These algorithms run locally and do not involve any network communication.

3.3 No External AI Services

Fox Reader does not use OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, or any other external AI service. There are no API calls to third-party AI providers.

4. Third-Party Services

Fox Reader integrates with the following third-party services:

4.1 Firebase Analytics (Google)

  • Provider: Google LLC
  • Purpose: Anonymous usage analytics to understand feature adoption and app performance.
  • Data Sent: Anonymous event data (e.g., "a book was imported," "a reading session ended") with non-identifying metadata (e.g., file type, word count, session duration). User properties include aggregate counts (total books, subscription status) that are not linked to your identity.
  • Data NOT Sent: Book titles, book content, personal notes, highlights, or any personally identifiable information.
  • Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy

4.2 Google Books API (Google)

  • Provider: Google LLC
  • Purpose: Fetching book metadata (title, author, description, page count, cover image) when you search for books or look up ISBNs.
  • Data Sent: Search queries (book titles, author names) and ISBN numbers you enter.
  • Data NOT Sent: Your reading progress, notes, highlights, or any personal data.
  • Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy

4.3 Open Library API (Internet Archive)

  • Provider: Internet Archive
  • Purpose: Supplementing book metadata, retrieving series information, subject classification, and book descriptions.
  • Data Sent: Book titles, author names, and ISBN numbers for lookup purposes.
  • Data NOT Sent: Your reading data, personal notes, or any personal information.
  • Privacy Policy: Internet Archive Privacy Policy

4.4 Bing News RSS (Microsoft)

  • Provider: Microsoft Corporation
  • Purpose: Displaying news articles about authors in your library.
  • Data Sent: Author names as search queries.
  • Data NOT Sent: Your reading data, book library contents, or personal information.
  • Privacy Policy: Microsoft Privacy Statement

4.5 Apple iCloud (CloudKit)

  • Provider: Apple Inc.
  • Purpose: Syncing your library data (books, highlights, notes, collections, reading progress, preferences) across your Apple devices.
  • Data Synced: All SwiftData model data and reader preferences, encrypted and stored in your personal iCloud account.
  • Control: iCloud sync is governed by your device's iCloud settings. You can disable iCloud sync for Fox Reader in your device's Settings app.
  • Privacy Policy: Apple Privacy Policy

4.6 Apple StoreKit 2

  • Provider: Apple Inc.
  • Purpose: Processing subscription purchases and verifying entitlements.
  • Data Handled: Subscription transactions are processed entirely by Apple. Fox Reader only reads the transaction status to determine whether premium features should be unlocked. We do not collect or store payment information.
  • Privacy Policy: Apple Privacy Policy

4.7 EPUBKit (Open Source)

  • Provider: Open-source library by Witek Bobrowski
  • Purpose: Parsing and rendering EPUB files locally on your device.
  • Data Handling: This library operates entirely offline and does not transmit any data.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information or reading data to any third party for commercial purposes.

5.2 We Do Not Share Your Data for Advertising

We do not share your data with advertisers or advertising networks. The App does not display ads.

5.3 Limited Data Transmission

The only data transmitted from your device is:

  • Anonymous analytics events to Firebase (not linked to your identity).
  • Book search queries (titles, authors, ISBNs) to Google Books API, Open Library API, and Bing News RSS when you actively initiate a search or lookup.
  • Library and preference data to your personal iCloud account if iCloud sync is enabled.
  • Subscription verification through Apple's StoreKit framework.

5.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request. However, because we do not collect personally identifiable information, the data we could provide in response to such requests is extremely limited.

6. Data Retention

6.1 On-Device Data

All user-generated data (books, highlights, notes, reading progress, vocabulary words, ratings, and collections) is stored on your device for as long as you use the App. This data is deleted when you uninstall the App or manually delete items within the App.

6.2 iCloud Data

Data synced to iCloud is retained in your personal iCloud account according to Apple's data retention policies. You can manage or delete this data through your iCloud settings.

6.3 Analytics Data

Firebase Analytics data is retained according to Google's data retention policies. This data is anonymous and cannot be linked back to you or your device. Firebase Analytics data is retained for a maximum of 14 months by default.

6.4 Cached Data

The App caches book metadata and news articles locally on your device to improve performance. This cached data is automatically managed and is removed when you uninstall the App.

7. User Rights

7.1 Access Your Data

All your data (books, highlights, notes, reading progress, ratings, and preferences) is stored on your device and is directly accessible within the App at any time.

7.2 Delete Your Data

You can delete your data in the following ways:

  • Individual Items: Delete specific books, highlights, notes, collections, or tracked books directly within the App.
  • Complete Deletion: Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.
  • iCloud Data: Manage or delete synced data through your device's iCloud settings (Settings > Apple Account > iCloud > Manage Storage).

7.3 Data Portability

Your reading data is stored locally on your device and can be accessed through standard iOS file management. Original book files (EPUB, PDF) remain in the App's document storage and are accessible via the Files app.

7.4 Opt Out of Analytics

You can limit analytics collection by enabling "Limit Ad Tracking" or disabling "Share Analytics" in your iOS device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements).

7.5 Contact Us

8. Children's Privacy

Fox Reader is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the App does not require account creation or collect personally identifiable information, there is minimal risk of inadvertent collection of children's data.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us at the email address listed above, and we will take steps to delete such information.

Fox Reader complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar international regulations regarding children's privacy.

9. Security Measures

We implement the following measures to protect your data:

  • On-Device Storage: All user data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework, which benefits from iOS's built-in hardware encryption and app sandboxing.
  • Encrypted Sync: iCloud data sync uses Apple's end-to-end encryption.
  • HTTPS: All network communications (Firebase Analytics, Google Books API, Open Library API, Bing News RSS) use HTTPS encryption for data in transit.
  • No Server Infrastructure: We do not operate backend servers that store your data. There is no centralized database of user information that could be breached.
  • App Sandboxing: iOS enforces strict app sandboxing, preventing other apps from accessing Fox Reader's data.
  • No User Accounts: The absence of user accounts, passwords, or login credentials eliminates an entire category of security risk.

10. International Data Transfers

Fox Reader is available globally on the Apple App Store. By using the App:

  • Analytics Data: Anonymous analytics data may be processed by Google (Firebase) on servers located in the United States or other countries where Google operates.
  • Book Metadata Requests: Search queries sent to Google Books API, Open Library API, and Bing News RSS may be processed on servers outside your country of residence.
  • iCloud Data: iCloud data is stored and processed by Apple according to Apple's data processing practices and applicable data transfer mechanisms.

All data transmitted internationally is protected by HTTPS encryption in transit. Because we do not collect personally identifiable information, the privacy impact of international data transfers is minimal.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or other jurisdictions with data protection laws: the anonymous analytics data processed by Firebase is covered by Google's data processing terms, which include Standard Contractual Clauses for international transfers.
